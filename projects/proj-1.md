**Sales Analysis by Research Field**

The aim of this initial project is to provide business insights into sales data for the company I work for by developing dashboards to analyse global sales across multiple company sites, integrating product, customer, licensing and commercial data to identify sales trends and portfolio opportunities. 

For this project, I will need to build relational data models and DAX measures to analyse performance by product family, research field, customer type and site.

The created dashboards are to be used by the CEO, CFO and Marketing Director.
